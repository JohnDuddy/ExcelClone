# Roadmap

Duddy Spreadsheet M12 is complete under the regression contract in `FEATURES.md`. The
following capabilities are explicitly outside the current product scope:

- VBA and macros
- Pivot tables
- Power Query
- Live market-data feeds
- Multi-variable optimization solver
- Real-time collaboration
- Embedded Office objects

They are candidates for a later major version and are not silently approximated
or dropped without warning.
